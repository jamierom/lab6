Jett

Duelist · Movement and Quick-entry. Jett is a fast-moving Duelist who creates space for her team to move onto sites.

Details

For Beginners: Practice confident movement and pay attention to how teammates follow an entry.

Sage

Sentinel · Support. Sage focuses on healing, slowing pressure and keeping a team organized.

Details

For Beginners: Watch your team's position and choose moments to support teammates and secure space.

Sova

Initiator · Scouting and Information. Sova helps his team gather information about nearby opponents.

Details

For Beginners: Share information clearly (using skill) before your teammates commit to a new area.

Brimstone

Controller · Space and Coordination. Brimstone helps the team manage sightlines and space by blocking smokes.

Details

For Beginners: Think about visibility and how to help teammates move together safely by smoking off opponents.

Killjoy

Sentinel · Area Awareness. Killjoy is known for tools that help monitor and protect sites from opponents.

Details

For Beginners: Pay attention to defensive coverage and call out what your team needs.

Raze

Duelist · Pressure. Raze is known for an energetic, space-making style of play from her bombs.

Details

For Beginners: Coordinate with teammates and focus on when to take entry.
